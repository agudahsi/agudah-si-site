About us

Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island

Known to Staten Islanders simply as “The Agudah.” An Orthodox congregation in the Willowbrook section of Staten Island, New York.

Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island — known to Staten Islanders and to visitors simply as The Agudah — is a shul in the plainest sense of the word: a place with minyanim every day, a beis medrash that stays lit, and a membership that carries the building. Most of what we do is measured in ordinary things: a minyan that makes it on a Tuesday morning in February, a family that gets meals for two weeks, a bochur who finds a chavrusa.

The Agudah is led by our rav, Rabbi Moshe Klein, shlita, who decides all matters of halacha and hashkafa for the kehillah.

Day-to-day, an elected board handles the shul’s affairs. The gabbaim manage the tefillos, aliyos and kibbudim; the treasurer handles membership, dues and the annual appeal.

Our organisation

Our legal name is Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island. We are a religious congregation incorporated in the State of New York and recognised by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit organisation. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

About this domain

agudah.si is the official internet domain of Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island. The domain was registered by, and is owned and controlled by, Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island. It is used for this website and for the organisation's official email addresses.

The domain reads as a contraction of our name: Agudah, which is what everyone here calls the shul, and SI for Staten Island. Although .si is administered as the country-code domain for Slovenia, it is openly registrable worldwide. We chose it because the two-letter suffix matches how our kehillah has abbreviated the shul’s name for years. Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island is located at 46 Birchard Avenue, Staten Island, New York 10314, United States, and has no operations in or connection to Slovenia.

The same congregation also maintains pages at shulspace.org/aysi and siagudah.weebly.com, both under the same organisation name and street address.

Membership

Membership covers a seat for the year, including the Yamim Noraim, and supports the shul's operating costs. Nobody is turned away for financial reasons; speak to the rav or the treasurer in confidence. To enquire about joining, email info@agudah.si.
