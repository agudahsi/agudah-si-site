Supporting the shul

The shul runs on membership dues, kiddush and shiur sponsorships, and the annual appeal. Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island is a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit organisation, and contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Ways to give

Online through our donation form, or by Zelle using the details below.

Cheques payable to Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island, mailed to the address at left or handed to the treasurer.

For sponsorships, standing orders or a gift of appreciated securities, email treasurer@agudah.si.

Pay by Zelle
Zelle ID
agudahsi

Before you send: confirm your banking app shows the recipient as Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island. If it shows any other name, stop and contact the treasurer. Zelle payments cannot be reversed.

When paying by Zelle, please put your name and what the payment is for — dues, a pledge, a kiddush — in the memo. A receipt is issued for every donation; if you need a prior year’s acknowledgement letter reissued, the treasurer can send one.
