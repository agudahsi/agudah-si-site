Text Message Consent

This form does one thing: it records that you would like the shul to text you. It is not a membership form and it is not required for anything. Joining the shul, paying dues and every other service are entirely separate, and none of them depend on your completing this.

Your details

What you are agreeing to

What we send
Account messages — statements, payment receipts, dues reminders, notice before an automatic payment is taken and if one fails, and confirmation of changes to your account. Congregational messages — changes to davening times, Yom Tov schedules, yahrzeit reminders, and notices about events and closures. We also reply by text when you ask us about your account or the schedule.
What we never send
No marketing. We do not buy phone numbers and we do not add anybody who has not given us his number for this purpose.
Message frequency
Frequency varies with your account. Typically a few messages a month.
Standard rates
The shul charges nothing for messages. Message and data rates may apply from your own mobile carrier.
Help and stop
Reply HELP to any message for help, or call the office on (718) 283-4280. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Stopping never affects your membership or your account.
Terms and privacy
Messaging terms at agudah.si/terms.html and privacy policy at agudah.si/privacy.html. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
