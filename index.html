The kehillah

What happens here

Most of what a shul does never makes it onto a website. What follows is the part that has a time attached to it.

Tefillah b'tzibbur

Shacharis every morning, with times set so people can daven before work. A late maariv at 10:00 PM Monday through Thursday.

Limud haTorah

Amud Yomi and Dirshu Mishna Berura through the week, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch on Sunday, Pirkei Avos over the summer and the Chaim Ozer father–son programme in the winter.

Chesed and support

Meals for families after a birth or a loss, a bikur cholim roster, and a discreet tzedakah fund administered by the rav.

Simchos and yahrtzeits

Aliyos, kibbudim and mi shebeirachs arranged through the gabbaim. Yahrtzeit reminders go out the week before.

The year

Coming up in the shul calendar

  • Weekly — Shalosh seudos Between mincha and maariv on Shabbos, with divrei Torah from the rav.
  • Monthly — Rosh Chodesh minyan Earlier shacharis to accommodate Hallel and krias haTorah.
  • Yamim Noraim Seating and membership arrangements open in Elul. Contact the office to reserve.
