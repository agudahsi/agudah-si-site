Zmanim and minyan times

We publish minyan times, zmanim and shiur schedules in good faith and keep them as current as we can. They change. A minyan moves for a levaya, a shiur shifts for Yom Tov, and the sheet on the door is the one that counts. Do not rely on this website for a time that matters — check with the shul.

Nothing on this site is a psak. Halachic questions go to our rav.

Text messages and WhatsApp

This is the programme we register with the mobile carriers, and these are its terms.

Who sends
Agudas Yisroel of Staten Island
What we send
Account messages to members who have given us a mobile number: notice that a statement is ready, receipts for payments, a warning before an automatic payment is taken, and yahrzeit reminders.
How often
Message frequency varies with your account. In an ordinary month it is none or one; it will not normally exceed a few messages a month.
Cost
We charge nothing for messages. Message and data rates may apply from your own mobile carrier.

How you opt in

You opt in by giving us your mobile number and agreeing to receive messages — on a membership form, at the office, or on the family screen of our member portal, where the choice is yours to set and is never ticked on your behalf. We do not buy phone numbers and we do not add anybody to this programme who has not given us their number for the purpose.

Consent to receive messages is never a condition of membership, of paying dues, or of anything else the shul does.

How you stop

Reply STOP to any message and we will stop sending them. You can also tell the office, or set your preferences in the member portal.

Texts and WhatsApp are separate consents. Replying STOP to a text stops texts and leaves WhatsApp alone; stopping WhatsApp leaves texts alone. Stop either, both, or neither.

Stopping messages does not affect your membership, your account, or anything else. We will still write to you by post or email where we have to.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, email info@agudah.si, or call (718) 283-4280.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier and your handset, and we cannot guarantee that any particular message arrives.

How we handle the information behind all of this — including the fact that mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes — is set out in our Privacy Policy.
