The weekly sheet

Mincha and maariv follow the zmanim and change every week. The current Zemanei Tefila sheet and the monthly shul calendar are posted at siagudah.weebly.com.

Where a time above says “see weekly sheet,” that sheet is the one to follow.

To sponsor a kiddush, arrange an aliyah, or add a name to the mi shebeirach list, speak to a gabbai or email gabbai@agudah.si.
